Tools ♡

Every tool on the road, sorted by what it does. Each stage page repeats the ones it needs, so you never have to come back here. You will anyway~

Rule one: a tool helps you read. It does not read for you. If you spend more time on setup than on the cat, close this page and go back to the cat ♡

Popup dictionaries

You point at a word, and the meaning appears. This is the tool that makes stage 2 possible. Set it up once, and then forget it exists.

Yomitan stage 2 onward
A browser extension. Hover a word on any web page, and press Shift. It shows the reading and the meaning. It needs dictionary files, which you import once.
Jitendex first dictionary
A free Japanese-to-English dictionary for Yomitan, built on JMdict with better formatting. Start with this one.
JMdict, JMnedict, and KANJIDIC for Yomitan add-ons
Fresh builds of the open dictionaries. JMnedict reads names. KANJIDIC explains single kanji. Add them after Jitendex.
More Yomitan dictionaries stage 3 onward
A list of extra dictionaries, with monolingual ones. When English definitions start to feel thin, add a Japanese-to-Japanese dictionary from here.
chibipop games and apps
A popup dictionary for text that is not in a browser. It reads the screen with OCR and shows definitions when you point at a word. It uses the same Yomitan dictionary files, and it can send words to Anki. Windows and Linux.

Yes, I wrote chibipop. No, that is not why it is here. Mostly~ (￣▽￣)

Web dictionaries

Use these when the popup is not enough, or when you want a second opinion.

Jisho stage 1 onward
Japanese to English. Search by kanji, kana, romaji, or English. It also breaks a whole sentence into words.
Kotobank stage 3 onward
Several Japanese dictionaries and encyclopedias in one search. All in Japanese. The best place to learn what a word means to Japanese people.
Weblio stage 3 onward
Japanese to Japanese, with a thesaurus and a classical Japanese dictionary. Useful again in stage 7.
goo辞書 stage 3 onward
Japanese to Japanese, clean and fast. It also lists four-character idioms and set phrases.
yoji.jitenon.jp stage 4
A dictionary of four-character idioms. Each entry gives the reading, the meaning, the origin, and similar idioms. Dive in and get lost.

Flashcards

Flashcards are optional on this road. The texts repeat the words for you. Use cards where the texts do not repeat enough: idioms, 漢文 characters, and tones.

Anki optional
Spaced-repetition flashcards. Free on desktop and Android. Keep each card small: one idiom, one reading, one meaning.
AnkiConnect optional
An Anki add-on. Yomitan and chibipop use it to make a card from the word under your cursor, in one click.

A thousand grammar cards and zero pages of the cat? I can see your deck from here~

Free texts

Everything on the road is free to read, except one light novel and one game.

Tae Kim's Guide to Learning Japanese stage 1
The grammar guide. Read it until you understand から and けど. Then stop.
Imabi stage 1 and later
A very large grammar reference, from beginner to classical Japanese. Do not read it in order. Look things up in it when a real sentence confuses you.
Aozora Bunko stages 2 and 7
A free library of Japanese books whose copyright has ended. The cat is here. Most of stage 7 is here too.
Japanese Wikipedia stages 5 to 7
More than a million articles. Also the easiest way to find the full text and the background of a classical work.
巫研 Docs Wiki (miko.org) stage 7
A wiki of out-of-copyright texts on folklore and literature, with authors such as 折口信夫 and 中山太郎. Old, dense, and strange in the best way.

Places to talk

Stage 3 needs people. These are the two easiest places to find Japanese speakers who will talk to a stranger.

VRChat stage 3
Free, and it works without a headset. Search for worlds with 日本 or 初心者 in the name, and join at Japan evening time. People talk to you because you are there.
Discord stage 3
Text and voice servers. Look for Japanese-English exchange servers, or Japanese servers about a hobby that you have. Write first. Voice later.

Scared of voice chat? Good. Type something, then. Anything. Go~ ♡
