The Arisu Method

This guide explains one way to learn Japanese from zero. It does not give you a checklist. It shows why each station on the line works.

The ride is hard on purpose. It follows your curiosity, and you own what you learn. Crawl if you must ♡

  • Love Line: the main route, stations 1 to 8
  • Sutra Line: station 5, beside stations 2 to 7
  1. 1
    AM01

    Kana and Tae Kim

    Learn just enough grammar to survive a sentence. Then stop.

  2. 2 AM02

    吾輩は猫である

    Start with a hard book on purpose. After it, every book feels lighter.
  3. 3
    AM03

    週に一度クラスメイトを買う話

    A story pulls you forward. Your own output shows you the gaps.

  4. 4
    AM04

    四字熟語

    Four kanji, one idea. Small idioms carry a lot of Japanese.

  5. 5
    AM05

    A slow thread of classical Chinese. It rides the Sutra Line beside stations 2 to 7.

  6. 6
    AM06

    Japanese Wikipedia

    Follow your interest. The links repeat words for you.

  7. 7
    AM07

    Really old texts

    Old Japanese builds stamina. After it, modern Japanese feels easy.

    The kanji you collected now carry over.
