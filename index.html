Welcome

The Arisu Method

This guide gives complete beginners a step-by-step plan for learning Japanese from zero.

The author learned Japanese with this plan. Each stage says what to do, why, and when to move on.

Ground rules

  • Start at stage 1. Go in order.
  • Crawl if you must. Slow progress is still progress.
  • Output until they understand. After stage 3, keep speaking and writing until people understand you.
  • Stage 5 runs alongside stages 2 to 7.
  • Do not give up.

Stages

  • 1. Kana and Tae Kim

    Learn kana. Then read Tae Kim’s grammar guide up to the lessons on から and けど.

  • 2. 我輩は猫である

    Open the novel. Crawl if you must.

  • 3. 週に一度クラスメイトを買う話

    Read the novel. Then start output, and keep going until people understand you.

  • 4. 四字熟語

    Look up four-character idioms in an online dictionary. Learn 200 to 300 of them, with their meanings.

  • 5. 般若心経 and 魏志倭人伝

    Read both texts along the way. Form your own interpretation.

    Runs alongside stages 2 to 7

  • 6. Japanese Wikipedia

    Read Japanese Wikipedia in large amounts.

  • 7. Really old texts

    Read really old texts. Crawl if you must. Do not give up.

  • 8. Chinese

    Start to learn Chinese. Your kanji knowledge gives you a head start.
