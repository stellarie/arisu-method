The Arisu Method ♡

This guide shows one way to learn Japanese from zero. It does not hand you a checklist. Each stage explains why it works, and what it feels like from the inside.

The method is hard on purpose. It follows your curiosity, and it makes you own what you learn. It does not look logical. Each hard part still has a reason. Crawl if you must ♡

  1. Kana and Tae Kim

    Learn just enough grammar to survive a sentence. Then stop.

    Yes, stop. You can do that, right?

  2. 吾輩は猫である

    Start with a hard book on purpose. After it, every book feels lighter.

    Too hard? That is the point~

  3. 週に一度クラスメイトを買う話

    A story pulls you forward. Your own output shows you the gaps.

    Now you talk. Try not to be boring~

  4. 四字熟語

    Four kanji, one idea. Small idioms carry a lot of Japanese.

    Look up 自業自得. It is about you~

  5. 般若心経 and 魏志倭人伝

    A slow thread of classical Chinese. Your own reading makes it stay.

    Your interpretation. Not the internet's~

    Runs beside stages 2 to 7

  6. Japanese Wikipedia

    Follow your interest. The links repeat words for you.

    Three hours on one article chain? Good~

  7. Really old texts

    Old Japanese builds stamina. After it, modern Japanese feels easy.

    Ancient grammar? You will cry. Cutely~

  8. Chinese

    The kanji that you collected now carry over.

    Congrats. Now do it all again~

Eight stages. No shortcuts. You can do it, probably~ (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

This guide describes one personal method. It is not a course.
