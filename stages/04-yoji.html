Summary

A four-character idiom is a set phrase of four kanji. In this stage you dive into an online dictionary of these idioms.

You learn 200 to 300 idioms by heart, with their meanings.

Do this

  1. Open the idiom dictionary site. Its address is yoji.jitenon.jp.
  2. Pick an idiom. Read its meaning and its origin.
  3. Follow links from one entry to the next.
  4. Learn each idiom by heart, with its meaning.
  5. Keep a list. Aim for 200 to 300 idioms.

Why

Each idiom packs a whole idea into four kanji. Many idioms come from old stories and texts.

You meet the same kanji again in later stages.

Move on when

  • You know 200 to 300 idioms by heart, with their meanings.
  • You pick any idiom from your list and give its meaning.

Pitfalls

  • Do not learn idioms from a bare list. Read each entry to see its origin.
  • Do not count an idiom until you know its meaning.
