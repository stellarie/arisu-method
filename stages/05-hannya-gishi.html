Summary

This stage runs alongside stages 2 to 7. You read two famous texts in small pieces as you go.

The texts are 般若心経, a Buddhist sutra, and 魏志倭人伝, a Chinese record of ancient Japan.

Do this

  1. Open the Japanese Wikipedia article for each text.
  2. Read both texts along the way, in small pieces.
  3. Crawl if you must. One line in a sitting counts.
  4. Form your own interpretation of each text. Write it in your own words.
  5. Read each text again later, when your Japanese is stronger.

Why

般若心経 is a short Buddhist sutra. Its text is classical Chinese. In Japan, people chant it straight through, with the Japanese reading of each character.

魏志倭人伝 is a passage of a Chinese history book about ancient Japan. It is also classical Chinese.

Both texts are hard. This is normal, and it is the reason to start early.

Move on when

  • You read a first pass of both texts.
  • You state your own interpretation of each text in a few sentences.

Pitfalls

  • Do not expect to understand every line. Both texts are hard.
  • Do not copy another reader's view. Form your own.

Your own view counts ♡ (｡-∀-)
