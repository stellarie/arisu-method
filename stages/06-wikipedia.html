Stage 6 of 8 · follow your curiosity

Japanese Wikipedia

Read Japanese Wikipedia about anything that you love, and follow the links. Your curiosity chooses the path, and the links repeat the words for you.

Why it works

  • Interest beats discipline

    You read far more when the topic is yours. Japanese Wikipedia has more than a million articles. Something on it is your favorite thing.

  • Links repeat vocabulary

    Articles on one topic share their words. When you follow the links, you meet the same terms again and again. This is repetition that you do not need to plan.

  • A new register

    Encyclopedia Japanese is formal and dense. It has its own sentence endings and its own vocabulary. After novels and old texts, this register completes your reading range.

What it feels like

It feels endless, in a good way. You start at one article and you end somewhere you did not expect.

The formal style feels stiff at first. After a few weeks, it feels clear and very efficient.

Lost in the links again? That is the method. You are welcome~ (｡-∀-)

Signs it is working

  • You read a full article without the dictionary.
  • You recognize the same technical words across different articles.
  • You open Japanese Wikipedia first, before the English one.
  • Your browser has far too many tabs open.

Forty tabs open? That is not a mess. That is progress~ ♡

Cute mistakes to skip

  • Do not read only the first paragraph. The good part is below it.
  • Do not pick topics because they look useful. Pick topics because you love them.
  • Do not switch to the English article when it gets hard.

Clicked over to English? I saw that~ (￣▽￣)
