Japanese Wikipedia

Summary

You read Japanese Wikipedia in large amounts. Pick topics that you like. Follow the links from page to page.

A long chain of links is the goal. It means that you keep reading.

Do this

  1. Open the Japanese Wikipedia site. Search for a topic you like.
  2. Read the article to the end. Look up the key words.
  3. Follow one link that looks interesting. Read that article next.
  4. Repeat. Go down the chain of links on purpose.
  5. Crawl if you must. Read a little and often.

Why

Japanese Wikipedia covers almost every topic. You can read about what you love, and interest keeps you reading.

Large amounts of reading build speed and a wide vocabulary.

Move on when

  • You read a full article on a topic you like in one sitting.
  • You follow links from article to article without a pause to give up.

Pitfalls

  • Do not stay on short, easy articles only. Try long ones.
  • Do not read only the first paragraph. Read to the end.

Happy reading ♡ (๑・∀・๑)
