Stage 8 of 8 · the bonus level

Chinese

Congratulations: you can start to learn Chinese now. The kanji and classical Chinese that you collected along the road carry over.

Why it works

  • The characters carry over

    Japanese and Chinese share many characters. Mainland China uses simplified forms, and Taiwan uses traditional forms. Either way, many meanings will already be familiar.

  • Classical Chinese carries over

    The idioms of stage 4 and the texts of stage 5 are classical Chinese. Modern Chinese grew from the same roots. You start with a deep background.

  • You know how to learn

    You crawled through hard texts and kept going. You know that curiosity carries you. That habit transfers to any language.

What it feels like

Reading feels strangely easy at first. You guess the meaning of many signs and headlines.

Pronunciation feels new. Chinese has tones, and its readings differ from the Japanese ones. This part starts from zero.

You thought you were finished? Cute. The road just got longer ♡

Signs it is working

  • You read a Chinese sentence and guess the topic from the characters.
  • You notice the link between a Japanese reading and a Chinese one.
  • You start this new road with the same curiosity as the first one.

Do not be that learner

  • Do not assume every shared character has the same meaning. Some differ.
  • Do not skip the tones. They are part of the word.
  • Do not forget your Japanese. Keep reading it too.
