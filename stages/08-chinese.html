Summary

You can start to learn Chinese now. This is the last stage of the roadmap.

It begins a new language project. Keep your Japanese reading going.

Do this

  1. Pick a Chinese course, textbook, or teacher.
  2. Learn how the sounds work before you read long texts.
  3. Compare the characters you know with Chinese characters. Note each difference.
  4. Keep your Japanese reading going at the same time.

Why

You now know many kanji. Chinese uses the same roots, so your kanji knowledge gives you a head start.

Move on when

  • You start regular Chinese study. No stage follows this one.
  • You keep reading Japanese while you study.

Pitfalls

  • Do not stop your Japanese reading. Keep both going.
  • Do not assume that a kanji means the same in both languages. Some differ.
