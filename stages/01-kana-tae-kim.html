Summary

This stage gives you the base. You learn the two kana sets, hiragana and katakana. Then you read Tae Kim's grammar guide.

Stop reading when you understand sentences with から and けど. The next stage starts real reading.

Do this

  1. Learn the hiragana chart. Write each kana by hand.
  2. Learn the katakana chart in the same way.
  3. Open Tae Kim's guide and start at the first lesson.
  4. Read the lessons in order. Do not skip ahead.
  5. Stop at the point where you understand から and けど.
  6. Crawl if you must. A slow day still counts.

Why

Kana show you how to say every Japanese word. Grammar gives you the rules that join words.

You need only the basics before real text. Real text teaches the rest.

Move on when

  • You read all hiragana and katakana without a chart.
  • You understand sentences that use から. This word gives a reason.
  • You understand sentences that use けど. This word contrasts two ideas.
  • You stop reading Tae Kim at that point.

Pitfalls

  • Do not read the whole guide first. Stop at the point above.
  • Do not wait to feel ready. Move on when the checks pass.
