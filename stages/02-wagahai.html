Stage 2 of 8 · your first real novel

吾輩は猫である

Start with a hard book on purpose. After it, every other book feels lighter.

Why it works

  • You learn to read with gaps

    Real Japanese always has words that you do not know. This book has a lot of them. It trains you to keep going anyway, and that skill matters more than any word list.

  • Your dictionary gets fast

    You look up many words on every page. Each lookup takes less time than the last one. After a few chapters, the dictionary feels like part of your hand.

  • Your scale resets

    夏目漱石 wrote this novel in 1905 and 1906, and its style is old and dense. After it, a modern novel feels easy. You start stage 3 with real confidence.

What it feels like

It feels slow. One page can take an hour, and some lines stay unclear after three readings. This is normal, and it is part of the method.

The first line helps you a little: 吾輩は猫である。名前はまだ無い。 The cat has no name yet, and you have no vocabulary yet. You are even.

Crawl if you must. One sentence in a day still counts. Even a cat moves faster than you, though~ ♡

Signs it is working

  • You look up fewer words on each page.
  • You guess meanings from context more often.
  • You stop panicking at old kanji. You just look them up.
  • You want to know what the cat does next.

Do not be that learner

  • Do not swap to an easier book after ten pages. The difficulty is the lesson.
  • Do not translate every sentence into English. Understand it, then move on.
  • Do not count the pages that are left. Count the pages that you finished.

The step: Read 吾輩は猫である from start to end, at any speed. The full text is free on Aozora Bunko.
