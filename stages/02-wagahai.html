Summary

You read your first real novel. Its title means “I Am a Cat”. 夏目漱石 wrote it in the early 1900s.

Do not expect to understand every line. You only need to keep going.

Do this

  1. Open the Aozora Bunko entry for the novel. It is free to read online.
  2. Start at the first sentence. Read in short sittings.
  3. Look up each word you do not know. Note its meaning.
  4. Crawl if you must. One sentence in a sitting still counts.
  5. Keep reading at your own pace.

Why

A real novel shows real Japanese. You meet common words many times, and repeated meetings make words stick.

The HTML edition on Aozora Bunko shows readings above many kanji. This helps a beginner.

Move on when

  • You finish the novel.
  • You keep reading even when you miss some words.

Pitfalls

  • Do not stop to master every line.
  • Do not quit in the first chapter. The start is the hard part.
