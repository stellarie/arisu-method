吾輩は猫である

A classic novel by 夏目漱石, told by a cat.

THE IDEAAM02 ●

Start with a hard book on purpose. After it, every other book feels lighter.

Why it works

  1. 1

    You learn to read with gaps

    Real Japanese always has words you do not know. This book trains you to keep going anyway.

  2. 2

    Your dictionary gets fast

    You look up many words on every page. Each lookup takes less time than the last one.

  3. 3

    Your scale resets

    After a hard classic, a modern novel feels easy. You arrive at station 3 with confidence.

What the ride feels like

It feels slow. One page can take an hour, and some lines stay unclear. This is normal, and it is part of the method.

Crawl if you must. One sentence in a day still counts ♡

Signs it is working

  • You look up fewer words on each page.
  • You guess meanings from context more often.
  • You want to know what the cat does next.

Your ticket · the step

Read 吾輩は猫である from start to end, at any speed. The full text is free on Aozora Bunko.
