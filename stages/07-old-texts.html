Summary

You read really old Japanese texts. The roadmap names no single book. Pick one that interests you.

Old texts use old grammar and old words. Crawl if you must. Do not give up.

Do this

  1. Pick one old text. Examples are 古事記, 枕草子, and 方丈記.
  2. Find the text on Aozora Bunko. Read its Japanese Wikipedia article first if you want context.
  3. Read a few lines. Look up each old word.
  4. Crawl if you must. Do not give up.

Why

Old texts show where modern Japanese comes from. They stretch your reading skill.

Modern text then feels easier.

Move on when

  • You read one old text to the end, or one full chapter of it.
  • You keep going when a line stays unclear.

Pitfalls

  • Do not give up when the text feels like a new language. That is normal.
  • Crawl if you must, but do not give up.
