Stage 3 of 8 · a story, then your own voice

週に一度クラスメイトを買う話

A story that you cannot put down teaches you fast. Then you speak and write, and you keep going until people understand you.

Why it works

  • The story pulls you

    This is a modern light novel with a lot of everyday dialogue. You read it because you want to know what happens next. Curiosity does the work that discipline cannot.

  • Modern Japanese, at last

    After the old style of stage 2, this book shows you how people talk now. You meet casual speech, short sentences, and real feelings. It fills the gap that the classic left.

  • Output finds your gaps

    When you speak or write, you find the words that you do not have. Reading alone never shows you this. Each gap that you find is a gap that you can now fill.

What it feels like

The book feels fast after stage 2. You turn pages at a speed that surprises you.

Output feels bad at first. People ask "what?" a lot. That is not their fault. If they do not understand you, it is your fault, so you try again in a different way.

Embarrassing? Very. You will survive. Probably~ ( ˙▿˙ )

Signs it is working

  • You read whole pages without the dictionary.
  • You use a phrase from the book in your own sentence.
  • People stop asking "what?" so often.
  • You notice your own mistakes before anyone points them out.

They understood you? Took you long enough~ ♡

Cute mistakes to skip

  • Do not wait for perfect Japanese before you output. Perfect never arrives.
  • Do not blame the listener. Change your words until they understand.
  • Do not stop output after one bad day. Keep going until they understand you.

Blaming the listener is the least cute thing you can do~

The step: Read 週に一度クラスメイトを買う話. Then start to speak and write in Japanese, and keep going until people understand you.
