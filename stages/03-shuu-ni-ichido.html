Stage 3 of 8 · a story, then your own voice

週に一度クラスメイトを買う話

A story that you cannot put down teaches you fast. Then you speak and write, and you keep going until people understand you.

Why it works

  • The story pulls you

    This is a modern light novel with a lot of everyday dialogue. You read it because you want to know what happens next. Curiosity does the work that discipline cannot.

  • Modern Japanese, at last

    After the old style of stage 2, this book shows you how people talk now. You meet casual speech, short sentences, and real feelings. It fills the gap that the classic left.

  • Output finds your gaps

    When you speak or write, you find the words that you do not have. Reading alone never shows you this. Each gap that you find is a gap that you can now fill.

What it feels like

The book feels fast after stage 2. You turn pages at a speed that surprises you.

Output feels bad at first. People ask "what?" a lot. That is not their fault. If they do not understand you, it is your fault, so you try again in a different way.

Embarrassing? Very. You will survive. Probably~ ( ˙▿˙ )

Signs it is working

  • You read whole pages without the dictionary.
  • You use a phrase from the book in your own sentence.
  • People stop asking "what?" so often.
  • You notice your own mistakes before anyone points them out.

They understood you? Took you long enough~ ♡

Cute mistakes to skip

  • Do not wait for perfect Japanese before you output. Perfect never arrives.
  • Do not blame the listener. Change your words until they understand.
  • Do not stop output after one bad day. Keep going until they understand you.

Blaming the listener is the least cute thing you can do~

Output: how to actually start

Reading fills you with Japanese. Output shows you where the holes are. You cannot see a hole until you fall into it. So you must talk to people, and you must fall in a lot.

Why real people, and not an app

An app forgives you. A person asks "what?". That "what?" is the whole lesson. It tells you exactly which sentence did not work, and it makes you try a second way on the spot. No exercise book does this.

VRChat

VRChat is free, and it works on a normal PC without a headset. Japanese users fill it every evening in Japan time. Search for worlds with 日本, 初心者, or 雑談 in the name. Stand near a group and say こんばんは. People talk to you because you are there and you tried. Nobody knows who you are. That makes the fear smaller.

  • Go at 21:00 to 01:00 Japan time. Earlier, the worlds are empty.
  • Say that you are learning. People slow down and help. They like it.
  • Do not switch to English when it gets hard. Say もう一回お願いします and try again.

Discord

Discord is text first, and text is kinder. Find a Japanese-English language exchange server, or a Japanese server about a hobby that you already have. Write short messages every day. Voice channels come later, when text feels easy.

  • Write about the thing you love. You already know the words from stage 2 and from this book.
  • Copy how natives write to each other. Casual Japanese on Discord is not textbook Japanese, and that is the point.
  • When someone corrects you, say thank you and use the correction in your next message.

How to not be boring

Have one thing to talk about. One game, one anime, one city you want to visit. A learner with a topic is fun to talk to. A learner who only asks "how do you say" is work.

Hiding in text chat for a month? Fine. Month two, you open the mic. I will know if you did not~ ( ˙▿˙ )

The step: Read 週に一度クラスメイトを買う話. Then start to speak and write in Japanese, and keep going until people understand you.

  • You finished the novel, and you want the next volume.
  • You have talked or written in Japanese with real people, more than ten times, and you kept going after the bad ones.
  • Someone understood you without a second try. Then it happened again.

They understood you twice? Fine. You may pass. Do not let it go to your head~ ♡
