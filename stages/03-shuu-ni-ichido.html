Summary

You read a modern light novel from start to end. After the book, you start output. Output means you speak and write Japanese yourself.

This guide quotes no passage from the book. Use the publisher link to find it.

Do this

  1. Find the novel on the publisher page. Buy or borrow a copy.
  2. Read the novel to the end. Crawl if you must.
  3. After the book, start output. Write short Japanese sentences, or speak with a person.
  4. If people do not understand you, keep output going until they do.
  5. Ask a reader or listener what was unclear. Then try again.

Why

Reading gives you input. Output tests what you know. A confused listener shows you a gap.

Move on when

  • You finish the novel.
  • People understand your spoken or written Japanese.

Pitfalls

  • Do not wait for perfect Japanese before you start output.
  • Do not copy long passages from the book into your notes.
